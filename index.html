    How to use Power Lab

    • Guide mode animates each wire. Practice mode asks you to click the two pins to connect yourself.
    • Click any glowing pin to inspect it. Use the toolbar to show all labels or preview upcoming wires.
    • Keys: ←/→ steps · P practice · L labels · A preview wires · O overview · C auto-camera · F fullscreen
    • The current values in the simulator are illustrative datasheet ballparks, not measurements.

    The PCB and enclosure layouts are simplified. The ICs, headers, connectors and switches are real KiCad 3D models. Always check pin labels on your actual hardware.
